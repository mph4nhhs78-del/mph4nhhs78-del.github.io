CLIPS

by Six Seven

Clips is a desktop tool for content creators. It turns long gaming streams into short vertical videos with subtitles and lets the creator review every clip before it is posted.

How it works

  1. The creator connects their own TikTok or Instagram account by signing in through the platform's official login.
  2. The app prepares short vertical clips with subtitles.
  3. The creator reviews each clip and approves or rejects it.
  4. Only approved clips are uploaded to the creator's own account through the official TikTok Content Posting API or Instagram API.

The app never posts anything without the creator's confirmation and only posts to accounts the creator has connected.
