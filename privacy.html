Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 16, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Clips (the "App"), provided by Six Seven ("we", "us"), handles information when you use it.

1. Information we access

When you connect a TikTok or Instagram account, the platform shares with the App only what you authorize, for example:

  • basic account information, such as your account ID, username, display name and avatar;
  • access tokens that allow the App to upload videos to your account.

The App also processes the video files that you choose to edit and publish.

2. How we use information

  • to show which account is connected;
  • to upload and publish the videos that you approve;
  • to show the status of your uploads.

We do not use your information for advertising and we do not sell it.

3. Where information is stored

Access tokens and settings are stored locally on the device where the App runs. Videos are processed locally and sent only to the platforms you choose, using their official APIs.

4. Sharing

We share data only with the platforms you connect (TikTok, Instagram/Meta), and only to perform the actions you request. We do not share your information with other third parties, except where required by law.

5. Retention and deletion

Information is kept only while your account is connected. You can disconnect at any time by removing the App's access in your TikTok or Instagram settings and by deleting the App's local data. After you disconnect, the App can no longer access your account.

6. Children

The App is not intended for children under 13 years old (or the minimum age required in your country), and we do not knowingly process their information.

7. Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy. The updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
